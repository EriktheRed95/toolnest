Text Sorter & Deduplicator

Sort lines of text alphabetically, reverse or shuffle them, and remove duplicate lines. Case-insensitive and trim options. Free, private, in-browser.

Sort, dedupe, and shuffle text lines

Paste any list with one item per line and reshape it in a click. Sort A→Z and Z→A order the lines alphabetically, Remove duplicates keeps only the first of each repeated line, Reverse flips the order, and Shuffle randomizes it fairly. Each button rewrites the text in place, so you can chain operations — dedupe, then sort, then copy the result.

Two options control how lines are compared. Case-insensitive treats "Apple" and "apple" as the same when sorting and deduplicating, instead of grouping all capitals first. Trim whitespace strips leading and trailing spaces so stray indentation doesn't hide duplicates or scramble the sort. Everything runs locally in your browser — no uploads, no limits — making this a fast way to clean up name lists, keywords, CSV columns, or any pile of text lines.

Frequently asked questions

How do I sort a list alphabetically?
Paste your list with one item per line, then click 'Sort A→Z' for ascending order or 'Sort Z→A' for descending. Turn on 'Case-insensitive' so that 'apple' and 'Apple' sort together rather than splitting upper and lowercase into separate groups.
How does removing duplicates work?
Click 'Remove duplicates' to keep only the first occurrence of each line, preserving the original order. With 'Case-insensitive' on, lines that differ only in capitalization count as duplicates; with 'Trim whitespace' on, surrounding spaces are ignored when comparing.
What does the shuffle button do?
Shuffle randomly reorders your lines using your browser's secure random generator and a Fisher–Yates shuffle, giving a fair, unbiased random order. It's handy for randomizing names, picks, or any list where you want no particular sequence.
