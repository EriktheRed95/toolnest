Tip Calculator

Calculate the tip and split the bill in seconds. Enter the bill, choose a tip percentage, and see the tip, total, and amount per person.

How to use the tip calculator

Enter your bill, tap a tip percentage (or type your own), and the calculator instantly shows the tip amount, the grand total, and — if you're splitting — what each person owes. Tick round up to land on a clean dollar total, which is handy when leaving cash.

As a quick guide for the U.S.: 15% is a minimum for sit-down service, 18–20% is the everyday standard, and 20–25% rewards great service. Counter service, delivery, and bars all have their own norms, so adjust to your situation.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I tip at a restaurant?
In the U.S., 18–20% of the pre-tax bill is standard for sit-down table service, 15% for adequate service, and 20%+ for great service. Use the preset buttons above to compare.
Should I tip on the pre-tax or post-tax amount?
Tipping on the pre-tax subtotal is customary, but many people tip on the total for simplicity. Either is acceptable — enter whichever bill figure you prefer.
How do I split a bill evenly with tip?
Enter the bill, set your tip percentage, and set the number of people. The calculator shows the total including tip and divides it equally so everyone pays the same amount.
