Random Picker & Decision Maker

Paste a list of names or options and let chance choose. Perfect for giveaways, raffles, picking a winner, or settling a decision. Free and instant.

What you can use it for

The random picker gives every line in your list an equal chance and selects one or more at random. Common uses: drawing winners for an Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube giveaway; running a classroom or team raffle; choosing who goes first; deciding where to eat; or assigning tasks fairly. Paste names from a spreadsheet or comments export — one per line — and you're set.

Keep No repeats ticked for giveaways so a single entry can't win multiple prizes. For a quick coin-flip style decision, just enter two options and pick one. The draw happens in your browser, so your list stays private.

Frequently asked questions

How do I pick a giveaway winner fairly?
Paste one entry per line, set how many winners you need, and press Pick. Each line has an equal chance. Tick "No repeats" so the same person can't win twice, and take a screenshot for transparency.
Can I pick more than one winner?
Yes — set "How many to pick" to the number you need. With "No repeats" on, the tool draws that many distinct entries.
Does it work for yes/no or either/or decisions?
Absolutely. Type your options (e.g. Yes / No, or restaurant names) on separate lines and pick one. It's a quick, unbiased way to break a tie.
