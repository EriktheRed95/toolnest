Why meaningless text is useful

Drop real writing into a draft layout and everyone in the room starts editing the words instead of reviewing the design. Lorem ipsum sidesteps that. Because it's neutral and unreadable, it keeps attention on the visual structure — column widths, line height, heading hierarchy, and how text wraps — while still mimicking the natural rhythm and word lengths of genuine prose.

The tradition stretches back to the 1500s, when a printer scrambled a passage of Cicero to make a type specimen, and it's been the publishing and web-design standard ever since. Frequently asked questions

What is lorem ipsum?
Lorem ipsum is scrambled, meaningless Latin-like text used as placeholder content in design and publishing. It lets you see how a layout looks with text in place before the real copy is written, without the distraction of readable words. It dates back to printing in the 1500s and remains the industry standard for dummy text.
Why use placeholder text instead of real words?
Real text pulls the eye and invites people to read and critique the writing during a design review. Lorem ipsum keeps the focus on layout, spacing, and typography by filling the space with neutral, non-distracting content that has a natural word-length distribution.
