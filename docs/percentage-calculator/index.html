Percentage formulas

A percentage is just a fraction out of 100. These are the three calculations people need most:

  • Percent of a number: X% of Y = Y × X ÷ 100. (20% of 150 = 30)
  • What percent: X is (X ÷ Y × 100)% of Y. (30 is 20% of 150)
  • Percent change: (New − Old) ÷ Old × 100. (150 → 180 is +20%)

Each calculator above updates as you type, so you can use it for tips, discounts, tax, grades, commissions, markups, or tracking how much a number went up or down.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?
Multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. For example, 20% of 150 is 150 × 20 ÷ 100 = 30. Use the first calculator above to do it instantly.
How do I find what percent one number is of another?
Divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100. For example, 30 out of 150 is 30 ÷ 150 × 100 = 20%. The second calculator does this for you.
How do I calculate percentage increase or decrease?
Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value, and multiply by 100. A rise from 150 to 180 is (180 − 150) ÷ 150 × 100 = +20%.
