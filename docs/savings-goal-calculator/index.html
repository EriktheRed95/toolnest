Savings Goal Calculator

Find out how much to save each month to reach a goal. Enter your target, current savings, timeline, and APY to see the monthly contribution and interest you'll earn.

Plan backward from your goal

Most savings advice tells you to "save more," but a specific goal deserves a specific number. This calculator works backward from your target: it grows whatever you've already saved at your APY, then solves for the monthly contribution needed to close the remaining gap by your deadline. Because each deposit earns interest from the day it lands, part of your goal is funded by the bank rather than by you — and the calculator shows exactly how much.

Three things move the monthly figure: the size of your goal, the time you give yourself, and the rate you earn. Stretching the timeline has the largest effect, because contributions get more compounding cycles. A higher-yield account helps too, though for short goals the difference is modest.

  • Automate the transfer — schedule the contribution the day after payday so it happens before you can spend it.
  • Use a high-yield account — a competitive APY lets interest do more of the work.
  • Give yourself room — a longer horizon lowers the monthly amount and eases the strain.

Estimate only. This assumes a constant APY and steady monthly contributions; savings rates change over time and aren't guaranteed. The APY is treated as the effective yearly yield, deposits are assumed to land at the end of each month, and taxes on interest are ignored. Use it as a target, not an exact schedule. Frequently asked questions

How does this savings goal calculator work?
It first grows your current savings at your APY over the time horizon, then figures out the extra monthly contribution needed to close any gap to your goal. The math uses the future-value-of-an-annuity formula: your APY is converted to an equivalent monthly rate, and each deposit, made at the end of a month, earns interest from then until your target date.
What APY should I use?
Use the rate your money will actually earn. High-yield savings accounts and money market accounts have recently paid in the 4-5% range, while regular savings accounts pay far less. For longer goals invested in the market, returns may be higher but aren't guaranteed. The 4% default reflects a competitive savings account.
What if my current savings already covers the goal?
If your existing balance, compounded at your APY, reaches the target on its own by the goal date, the calculator tells you no further saving is required. You can then redirect what you would have saved toward another goal or simply give yourself breathing room.
How can I reach my goal faster?
Three levers control the monthly amount: a smaller target, a longer timeline, or a higher return. Extending the deadline has an outsized effect because contributions have more time to compound. Automating transfers also helps, since money saved before you see it is money you won't accidentally spend.
