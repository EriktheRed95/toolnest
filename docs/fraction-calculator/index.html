Fraction Calculator

Add, subtract, multiply, and divide fractions. Get the simplified result, its decimal value, and the mixed-number form, all calculated instantly.

Working with fractions

Fraction arithmetic trips up a lot of people because the rules differ for each operation. To add or subtract, you first need a common denominator; to multiply, you multiply across the tops and bottoms; and to divide, you flip the second fraction and multiply. This calculator handles all four and then reduces the answer to lowest terms automatically, so you never have to spot the common factor yourself.

Each result comes in three forms so you can use whichever fits: the simplified fraction, its decimal equivalent, and — when the answer is improper — the mixed number. Negative values and sign handling are taken care of, and the tool guards against impossible inputs like a zero denominator or dividing by a fraction that equals zero.

  • Add / subtract — needs a common denominator, done for you.
  • Multiply — numerators times numerators, denominators times denominators.
  • Divide — invert the second fraction, then multiply.

Frequently asked questions

How does the calculator simplify fractions?
After computing the result, it finds the greatest common divisor (GCD) of the numerator and denominator using Euclid's algorithm, then divides both by it. That reduces the fraction to its lowest terms — for example, 6/8 becomes 3/4.
What is a mixed number?
A mixed number combines a whole number and a proper fraction, like 1 1/4. When a result is improper (the numerator is larger than the denominator), the tool also shows the mixed-number form alongside the simplified fraction.
Can it handle negative fractions?
Yes. Enter a negative numerator or denominator and the calculator tracks the sign correctly. The result keeps the sign on the numerator and shows a leading minus where appropriate.
What happens if I divide by zero?
A fraction can't have a denominator of zero, and you can't divide by a fraction equal to zero. If either situation comes up, the calculator flags it instead of showing a result.
