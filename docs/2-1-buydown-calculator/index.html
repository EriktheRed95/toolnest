2-1 Buydown Calculator

See your reduced mortgage payment in years 1 and 2 of a 2-1 temporary buydown, the monthly savings, and the upfront buydown cost.

How a 2-1 buydown lowers your first two years

A 2-1 temporary buydown shaves 2 percentage points off your mortgage rate in the first year and 1 point in the second, before settling at the full note rate from year 3 onward. The loan amount, term, and note rate never change — only the rate used to figure your payment during the buydown period. The result is a noticeably smaller payment early on, when a new mortgage often feels tightest.

The savings aren't free. Someone funds the gap upfront — usually the seller or builder as a concession — and the lump sum is parked in an escrow account that tops up each month's payment to the full amount. This calculator adds up those twelve monthly differences in year 1 and year 2 to show the total buydown cost.

  • Best when you expect rising income or plan to refinance before year 3.
  • Remember lenders qualify you at the full note rate, not the reduced one.

Estimate only. Actual buydown structures, escrow handling, and qualifying rules vary by lender and program. This is not financial advice.

Frequently asked questions

How does a 2-1 buydown work?
A 2-1 buydown temporarily lowers your mortgage rate by 2 percentage points in year 1 and 1 point in year 2, then returns to the full note rate from year 3 through the end of the loan. The loan balance and term don't change — only the interest rate used for the first two years' payments does.
Who pays for a 2-1 buydown?
The cost is usually paid upfront as a lump sum, most often by the seller or builder as a concession, though a buyer or lender can fund it too. The money is held in escrow and used to cover the gap between your reduced payment and the full payment each month for the first two years.
What happens in year 3?
The temporary subsidy ends and your payment rises to the full note-rate amount for the rest of the loan. Lenders qualify you at the full note rate, so you must be able to afford that higher payment — the buydown only eases the first two years.
Is a buydown the same as buying points?
No. Discount points permanently lower your rate for the life of the loan. A 2-1 buydown is temporary, affecting only years 1 and 2. Buydowns help most when you expect income to rise or plan to refinance, while points suit a long hold at a fixed rate.
