Magic 8-Ball — Ask a Question

Ask the online Magic 8-Ball a yes-or-no question and get one of the 20 classic answers. A fun, random fortune-telling toy that runs in your browser.

Ask a question…

Ask the Magic 8-Ball

Think of a yes-or-no question, type it in if you like, and tap the button to receive your fortune. The 8-Ball draws from the 20 classic answers — ten encouraging, five non-committal, and five discouraging — picked at random with your browser's cryptographically secure generator. Every shake is independent, so you can ask the same question twice and get a different reply, exactly like the toy that's been settling playground debates since 1950.

It's a novelty, of course: the 8-Ball has no idea what your future holds, and "Outlook good" carries no more weight than the flip of a coin. That's the whole charm — it takes the pressure off a trivial decision and hands it to chance. Use it for icebreakers, party games, picking where to eat, or just for a little fun. For the choices that actually matter, trust your own judgment instead.

Frequently asked questions

How does the Magic 8-Ball work?
Think of a yes-or-no question, then tap the button. The 8-Ball randomly picks one of the 20 classic replies — ten positive, five non-committal, and five negative — using your browser's secure random generator. Just like the real toy, the answer is pure chance.
What are the 20 possible answers?
They range from confident yeses like 'It is certain' and 'Without a doubt' to hedges like 'Reply hazy, try again' and 'Ask again later', and firm noes like 'Don't count on it' and 'Very doubtful'. Ten are affirmative, five are neutral, and five are negative.
Should I make real decisions with it?
It's for fun. The 8-Ball is a novelty toy with no insight into your future — it's a random reply, nothing more. Enjoy it for games, icebreakers, and settling silly debates, but make important choices the old-fashioned way.
