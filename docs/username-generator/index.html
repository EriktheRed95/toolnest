Username Generator

Generate fun, memorable usernames by combining an adjective and a noun, with optional numbers and capitalization. Cryptographically random and private.

Find a username you actually like

Coming up with a good handle on the spot is hard. This generator does the brainstorming for you by stitching together a random adjective and noun — pairings like swiftfalcon, lunarpanda, or turbogecko — that read well and are easy to remember. Toggle Add numbers to tack on a couple of digits, which makes it far more likely a name is still free, and toggle Capitalize for a CamelCase look.

Slide to choose how many ideas you want and hit Generate for a fresh batch any time; copy the whole list with one click. The names are built with your browser's cryptographically secure randomness, entirely on your device, so nothing is uploaded or stored. Try a few on the site you're signing up for and pick the one that fits.

Frequently asked questions

How are the usernames made?
Each username pairs a random adjective with a random noun — like swiftfalcon or lunarpanda — and can append a two- or three-digit number. You can choose whether words are capitalized. The randomness comes from your browser's secure crypto generator.
Will these usernames be available?
This tool generates ideas; it can't check whether a name is already taken on a given site. Adding numbers greatly increases the odds of finding a free variant. Generate a fresh batch and try a few on the service you have in mind.
Are the generated names private?
Yes. Everything is created locally in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere or saved. Reload the page and you get a brand-new set with no history kept.
