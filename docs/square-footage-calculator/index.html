Square Footage Calculator

Calculate square footage from length and width in feet. See the area in square feet, square yards, and square meters, plus total cost from a price per square foot.

How to calculate square footage

Enter the length and width of a space in feet and the calculator multiplies them to give the area in square feet, then converts it to square yards and square meters. For an L-shaped or irregular room, split it into rectangles, measure each one, and add the results together.

Add an optional price per square foot to estimate material cost — useful for flooring, carpet, tile, paint, or any project priced by area. Square footage is also the standard way real estate listings describe a home's size. Whatever you're measuring, it's smart to buy 5–10% extra material to cover offcuts, breakage, and mistakes, so your project doesn't stall waiting on one more box.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate square footage?
Multiply the length in feet by the width in feet. For a room 12 ft long and 10 ft wide, the area is 12 × 10 = 120 square feet. This calculator does the math instantly and also converts to square yards and square meters.
How do I convert square feet to square yards or square meters?
Divide square feet by 9 to get square yards, since a square yard is 3 ft by 3 ft. Multiply square feet by 0.092903 to get square meters. The calculator shows both conversions automatically.
How do I estimate material cost from square footage?
Enter a price per square foot and the tool multiplies it by the total area. This gives a quick estimate for flooring, tile, carpet, or paint. Add 5–10% extra for waste, cuts, and mistakes.
