How to calculate square footage

Enter the length and width of a space in feet and the calculator multiplies them to give the area in square feet, then converts it to square yards and square meters. For an L-shaped or irregular room, split it into rectangles, measure each one, and add the results together.

Add an optional price per square foot to estimate material cost — useful for flooring, carpet, tile, paint, or any project priced by area. Square footage is also the standard way real estate listings describe a home's size. Whatever you're measuring, it's smart to buy 5–10% extra material to cover offcuts, breakage, and mistakes, so your project doesn't stall waiting on one more box.
