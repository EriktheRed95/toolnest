Why meaningless text is useful

Drop real writing into a draft layout and everyone in the room starts editing the words instead of reviewing the design. Lorem ipsum sidesteps that. Because it's neutral and unreadable, it keeps attention on the visual structure — column widths, line height, heading hierarchy, and how text wraps — while still mimicking the natural rhythm and word lengths of genuine prose.

The tradition stretches back to the 1500s, when a printer scrambled a passage of Cicero to make a type specimen, and it's been the publishing and web-design standard ever since.
