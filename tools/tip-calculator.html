How to use the tip calculator

Enter your bill, tap a tip percentage (or type your own), and the calculator instantly shows the tip amount, the grand total, and — if you're splitting — what each person owes. Tick round up to land on a clean dollar total, which is handy when leaving cash.

As a quick guide for the U.S.: 15% is a minimum for sit-down service, 18–20% is the everyday standard, and 20–25% rewards great service. Counter service, delivery, and bars all have their own norms, so adjust to your situation.
