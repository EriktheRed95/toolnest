What you can use it for

The random picker gives every line in your list an equal chance and selects one or more at random. Common uses: drawing winners for an Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube giveaway; running a classroom or team raffle; choosing who goes first; deciding where to eat; or assigning tasks fairly. Paste names from a spreadsheet or comments export — one per line — and you're set.

Keep No repeats ticked for giveaways so a single entry can't win multiple prizes. For a quick coin-flip style decision, just enter two options and pick one. The draw happens in your browser, so your list stays private.
