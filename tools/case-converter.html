Convert text to any case

Retyping text just to fix its capitalization is a waste of time. Paste it here and switch instantly between the cases you need — from a headline in Title Case to an all-caps heading, a clean Sentence case paragraph, or the programmer-friendly camelCase, snake_case, and kebab-case formats. Each button transforms the text in place; copy the result with one click.

Which case should you use?

  • UPPERCASE — emphasis, acronyms, or headings. Use sparingly; all-caps is harder to read in long stretches.
  • lowercase — a uniform, casual look, or to normalize text before processing.
  • Title Case — headlines, titles, and headings where each major word is capitalized.
  • Sentence case — normal prose: capitalize the first word and proper nouns only.
  • camelCase / snake_case / kebab-case — variable and file names in code, URLs, and CSS classes. Pick the convention your project uses.
