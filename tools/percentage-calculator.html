Percentage formulas

A percentage is just a fraction out of 100. These are the three calculations people need most:

  • Percent of a number: X% of Y = Y × X ÷ 100. (20% of 150 = 30)
  • What percent: X is (X ÷ Y × 100)% of Y. (30 is 20% of 150)
  • Percent change: (New − Old) ÷ Old × 100. (150 → 180 is +20%)

Each calculator above updates as you type, so you can use it for tips, discounts, tax, grades, commissions, markups, or tracking how much a number went up or down.
