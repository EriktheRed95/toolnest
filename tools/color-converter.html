Convert colors between formats

Designers and developers juggle the same color in several notations. A design tool hands you a HEX code, a stylesheet might use rgb(), and you'll often reach for HSL when you want to nudge a hue or lightness by hand. This converter shows all three at once: type a hex value or pick a color visually, and the swatch plus the RGB and HSL strings update instantly.

Each output is a ready-to-paste CSS value, and the copy buttons grab the rgb() or hsl() string in one click. The hex field and the color picker stay locked together, so whichever you change, every representation follows. It all runs locally in your browser, with no uploads and nothing stored.
