The exact span between two dates

Whether you're counting down to an event, measuring how long a project ran, or working out someone's tenure, this calculator gives the precise gap between any two dates. It breaks the difference into years, months, and days by reading the actual calendar — so the uneven lengths of months and every leap day are counted correctly rather than averaged away.

Alongside the calendar breakdown, it converts the same span into total days, weeks, hours, and minutes. These figures are handy for billing, deadlines, or simply satisfying curiosity about how much time has really passed.

Order doesn't matter

You can put the later date first or second — the calculator always shows the absolute difference and simply notes when it has swapped the inputs for you. Both fields default to today, so you can change just one date to measure forward or backward from now. Everything recalculates the instant you change a field, making it quick to compare several spans in a row.
