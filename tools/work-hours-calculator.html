From time card to take-home

This calculator turns a pair of clock times into the numbers you actually need: hours and minutes worked, decimal hours, and gross pay. Enter when you clocked in and out, subtract any unpaid break, and the result appears instantly. Decimal hours matter because most payroll systems want them — eight and a half hours becomes 8.50, not "8h 30m."

Add your hourly rate and the tool multiplies it by your decimal hours to estimate gross pay for the shift. Leave the rate at zero if you only need the hours.

Breaks and overnight shifts

If your meal break is unpaid, enter its length in minutes and it's deducted from your total; set it to zero when all breaks are paid. The calculator also handles shifts that cross midnight: when the clock-out time is earlier than the clock-in time, it assumes you worked into the next day and adds 24 hours automatically, so a 10 PM to 6 AM shift reads as a clean eight hours.

Gross pay is an estimate before taxes, overtime rules, and other deductions, which vary by employer and location.
