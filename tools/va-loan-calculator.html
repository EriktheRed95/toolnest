Why VA loans are different

VA loans are backed by the Department of Veterans Affairs for eligible veterans, active service members, and certain surviving spouses. Two features set them apart: you can buy with zero down, and there's no monthly mortgage insurance — ever. That second point is a real monthly savings compared with FHA or low-down conventional loans, which tack on mortgage insurance for years.

In place of ongoing insurance, VA loans charge a one-time funding fee, a percentage of the loan that most borrowers finance into the balance rather than pay in cash. The fee helps sustain the program. This calculator builds your payment from principal and interest plus property taxes and homeowners insurance.

  • Funding fee waivers apply to many veterans receiving disability compensation — set it to 0% to model that.
  • Putting money down is optional but lowers both your loan and your funding fee.

Estimate only. Funding fee percentages depend on down payment, service type, and whether it's your first use. Confirm your fee and eligibility with a VA-approved lender. This is not financial advice.
