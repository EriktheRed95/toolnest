Estimate your nest egg

This calculator projects how large your retirement savings could grow by the time you stop working. It takes your current balance and adds your monthly contributions, compounding everything monthly at your expected return until your chosen retirement age. The result shows not just the final figure, but how much of it came from your own deposits versus how much compounding added on top.

The single most powerful variable is time. Money you invest in your thirties has decades of compounding cycles ahead of it; the same dollars added in your fifties simply have fewer. That's why even modest contributions, started early and left alone, often end up dwarfing larger contributions made later.

  • Contribute consistently — automatic monthly deposits keep compounding working without relying on willpower.
  • Capture any employer match — a 401(k) match is an immediate, guaranteed return you don't want to leave behind.
  • Stay invested through dips — long horizons smooth out year-to-year swings.

Estimate only. This assumes a steady annual return, but real investments fluctuate and aren't guaranteed. It ignores inflation, taxes, and fees, which reduce real-world results, and doesn't account for Social Security or pensions. Treat it as a projection, not a promise. This is not financial advice.
