Know exactly what you need

Heading into finals, the question on everyone's mind is simple: what do I need to score to hit my goal? This calculator answers it. Give it your current grade, how much the final is worth toward your overall mark, and the grade you're aiming for, and it solves for the exact score the exam requires. The result updates live, so you can try different target grades and see what each one demands.

The math weights your existing grade by everything that isn't the final, then works out how high the final must be to pull your total up to your target. It's the same calculation a professor uses, just instant.

Reading the result

Two outcomes are worth watching for. If the needed score comes out above 100%, your target isn't reachable through the final alone — time to either lower the goal or look for extra credit. If it comes out at zero or below, congratulations: you've already secured your desired grade and could skip the exam (figuratively) without dropping below it. Anything in between is your real target.

This assumes a standard weighted average and a 100-point final. Confirm your course's exact grading policy, since curves and bonus points can shift the result.
