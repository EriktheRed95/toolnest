What you'll actually pay

A "25% off" tag is easy; the real total gets murkier once a second markdown or sales tax enters the picture. This calculator gives you the bottom line — the sale price, your total savings, and the out-the-door price after tax. To find a sale price by hand, multiply the original by one minus the discount: a 25% discount means you pay 75%, so $80 × 0.75 = $60.

The stacked-discount trap

"Take an extra 10% off sale prices!" sounds like 30% when the rack already says 20% off — but it isn't. Extra discounts apply to the already-reduced price, so they multiply rather than add: 20% then 10% is 0.80 × 0.90 = 0.72, a 28% total reduction. The more discounts stack, the bigger the gap between the advertised sum and the real savings. This tool applies each discount in sequence so you see the true effective percentage.

Sales tax then lands on the final discounted amount, since that's what you actually pay. Add your local rate above for the genuine total.

Store policies vary — some coupons apply to the original price, and a few items are excluded from stacking. Use this for quick comparisons, and check the fine print at checkout.
