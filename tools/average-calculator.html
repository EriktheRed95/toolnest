Find the mean, median, and mode

Paste a list of numbers — separated by commas, spaces, or new lines — and the calculator instantly works out the three most common measures of center. The mean is the everyday average. The median is the middle value, which resists outliers. The mode is the most frequent value, and the tool flags when several tie or when there's no mode at all. Non-numeric text is ignored, so you can paste messy data without cleaning it first.

Alongside the averages you also get the count, sum, minimum, maximum, and range, giving a quick snapshot of how your data is spread. This is handy for grading, budgeting, lab measurements, sports stats, or any time you need to summarize a set of numbers. Everything runs in your browser and recalculates live as you edit, so it stays fast and completely private.
