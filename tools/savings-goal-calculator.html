Plan backward from your goal

Most savings advice tells you to "save more," but a specific goal deserves a specific number. This calculator works backward from your target: it grows whatever you've already saved at your APY, then solves for the monthly contribution needed to close the remaining gap by your deadline. Because each deposit earns interest from the day it lands, part of your goal is funded by the bank rather than by you — and the calculator shows exactly how much.

Three things move the monthly figure: the size of your goal, the time you give yourself, and the rate you earn. Stretching the timeline has the largest effect, because contributions get more compounding cycles. A higher-yield account helps too, though for short goals the difference is modest.

  • Automate the transfer — schedule the contribution the day after payday so it happens before you can spend it.
  • Use a high-yield account — a competitive APY lets interest do more of the work.
  • Give yourself room — a longer horizon lowers the monthly amount and eases the strain.

Estimate only. This assumes a constant APY and steady monthly contributions; savings rates change over time and aren't guaranteed. The APY is treated as the effective yearly yield, deposits are assumed to land at the end of each month, and taxes on interest are ignored. Use it as a target, not an exact schedule. This is not financial advice.
