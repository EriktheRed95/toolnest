How a 2-1 buydown lowers your first two years

A 2-1 temporary buydown shaves 2 percentage points off your mortgage rate in the first year and 1 point in the second, before settling at the full note rate from year 3 onward. The loan amount, term, and note rate never change — only the rate used to figure your payment during the buydown period. The result is a noticeably smaller payment early on, when a new mortgage often feels tightest.

The savings aren't free. Someone funds the gap upfront — usually the seller or builder as a concession — and the lump sum is parked in an escrow account that tops up each month's payment to the full amount. This calculator adds up those twelve monthly differences in year 1 and year 2 to show the total buydown cost.

  • Best when you expect rising income or plan to refinance before year 3.
  • Remember lenders qualify you at the full note rate, not the reduced one.
