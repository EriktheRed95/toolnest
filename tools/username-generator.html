Coming up with a good handle on the spot is hard. This generator does the brainstorming for you by stitching together a random adjective and noun — pairings like swiftfalcon, lunarpanda, or turbogecko — that read well and are easy to remember. Toggle Add numbers to tack on a couple of digits, which makes it far more likely a name is still free, and toggle Capitalize for a CamelCase look.

Slide to choose how many ideas you want and hit Generate for a fresh batch any time; copy the whole list with one click. The names are built with your browser's cryptographically secure randomness, entirely on your device, so nothing is uploaded or stored. Try a few on the site you're signing up for and pick the one that fits.
