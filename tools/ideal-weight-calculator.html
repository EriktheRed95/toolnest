What "ideal weight" really means

Ideal body weight formulas estimate a reference weight from your height and sex. The best-known — Devine, Robinson, and Miller — were developed decades ago, originally to help calculate medication doses, and each returns a slightly different figure. Showing all three together makes it obvious that there is no single magic number; rather, there is a reasonable band of healthy weights.

That's why this calculator also displays the healthy weight range from body mass index, the span of weights that puts your BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 for your height. A range is usually more useful than a single target because it acknowledges natural variation in build and body composition. None of these methods account for muscle mass, frame size, or age.

  • Devine — the most commonly cited formula in clinical settings.
  • Robinson — tends to produce slightly lower estimates.
  • Miller — tends to produce slightly higher estimates.

Not medical advice. These are rough estimates for general information only and aren't a substitute for evaluation by a qualified healthcare provider.
