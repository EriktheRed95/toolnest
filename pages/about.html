ToolNest is a collection of free online calculators and tools built around one idea: each tool should do a single job well, load fast, and work on any device without a sign-up or clutter.

We focus on the calculations people actually need day to day — from working out a mortgage payment or what you'll net from selling a home, to everyday math like percentages and tips, to quick random tools for giveaways and decisions. Every tool runs right in your browser, so your inputs stay on your device.

The site is independently run and supported by advertising, which lets us keep every tool free to use. If you have a suggestion for a tool you'd like to see, we'd love to hear it — see our contact page.

Our principles

  • Free — no paywalls, no accounts.
  • Fast — lightweight pages that load quickly on mobile.
  • Private — calculations happen in your browser; we don't collect the numbers you enter.
  • Clear — plain-English explanations alongside every tool.
